# SOXLUSDT ATR Parameter Sensitivity

## Method

This is a same-period sensitivity test from `2026-08-09 00:00 UTC` through the
latest complete local tick on `2026-08-26`. Every candidate uses the current
SOXLUSDT live strategy structure: 15-minute tick ATR, long-only, trend efficiency
8 / 0.25, 0.25 ATR reversal confirmation, no profit protection, no continuation
re-entry, 2x isolated leverage with 62.5% position allocation, 5 bps fees, 2 bps
slippage, and recorded funding. Live startup alignment is disabled.

The scan covers 35 combinations: ATR periods 14, 21, 28, 32, 35, 42, 56 and
multipliers 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0. This is parameter sensitivity, not a new
walk-forward selection; the period is too short to justify changing production
parameters.

better.

## Decision

Keep the frozen ATR(32) × 3.0 production parameters unchanged. Do not select ATR(21)
× 2.5 from this short, same-period sample and do not increase leverage. Continue
forward observation with a pre-declared parameter set and evaluate only after a
larger independent sample.
