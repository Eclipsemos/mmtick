The authoritative run is
[`monthly-target-feasibility-20260815-163233-907206.md`](monthly-target-feasibility-20260815-163233-907206.md),
with adjacent machine-readable JSON. It separates January-July 2026 from partial August and
replays both base and stress transaction costs.

monthly locks were chosen after inspecting 2026. A 2021-2025 reverse audit finds zero risk-eligible
configurations across the positive 5%-step weight grid at 2x-8x leverage. The development-risk-
controlled version reaches only 3/7 complete 2026 months. The study is rejected, is not approved
for trading, and does not achieve the research goal.
